# Home feed

The home page opens with library counts (papers, PDFs, PDF coverage, categories, tags) and then the home feed.

![Home page with counts and feed](../assets/screenshots/home.png)

## Feeds

The feed shows entries from up to 10 RSS or Atom feeds, for example `https://rss.arxiv.org/rss/cs.LG` or a bioRxiv subject feed. Add, rename, switch off or remove feeds in **Settings → Library → Home feed**. The cache retention setting there controls how many days of feed entries stay stored; papers you dismiss for good are always remembered.

The picker above the list shows all feeds merged, a random 10 entries, or one feed at a time. The keyword box filters the list to entries that contain every one of the comma-separated keywords.

Save and Dismiss work on arXiv entries only.

## Filter rules

Filter rules hide entries before you see them. A DENY rule hides an entry when all its comma-separated keywords appear in the chosen field (title or summary). An ALLOW rule overrides a DENY match. Rules apply to every feed and live in **Settings → Library → Feed filters**.

## Saved PDFs view

Home also has a Saved PDFs view that lists 10 to 100 saved PDFs at random, by size, or only untagged ones.
